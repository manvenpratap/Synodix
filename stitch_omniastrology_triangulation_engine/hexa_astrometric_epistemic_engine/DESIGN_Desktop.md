## Brand & Style

The design system establishes the visual and structural language for a multi-tradition astrometric computation platform that evaluates celestial coordinates across six disparate cosmological and mathematical traditions: Hellenistic, Jyotisha, BaZi, Zi Wei Dou Shu, Maya Cholq'ij, and Tibetan Kalachakra.

The design identity fuses **Deep Astrometric Observatory** instrumentation with **Precision Scientific Telemetry**. Rather than evoking esoteric or mystical tropes, the interface functions as a rigorous epistemological tool—approaching astronomical charts with the exactitude of mission control consoles, orbital mechanics terminals, and statistical Bayesian confidence systems.

### Visual Principles
- **Instrument Precision:** Information density is high, deliberate, and tabular. Surfaces exhibit structural gridlines, subtle coordinate graticules, and micro-metric calibrations.
- **Epistemic Clarity:** Data states prioritize mathematical certitude, divergence markers, and confidence intervals over decorative embellishment.
- **Observatory Immersion:** Deep stratified abyssal surfaces recede into the canvas, allowing primary starlight brass accents and JPL cyan vector telemetry to command foreground focus.
- **Glass-Slate Architecture:** Panels maintain crisp structural integrity with hairline borders, translucent slate layers, and controlled ambient celestial backdrops.

## Colors

The system uses a dark-first color space modeled on deep-space observatory consoles and orbital trajectory visualizers. The palette relies on specific chromatic assignments:

- **Abyss Black Base (`#080B10`, `#0D1118`, `#131A24`):** Canvas backgrounds and deep recess wells that absorb visual noise and provide infinite contrast for tabular coordinates.
- **Observatory Slate (`#161F2E`, `#1E293B`, border `#2B384E`):** Multi-tiered card surfaces, computational modules, and instrument panels.
- **Starlight / Brass Gold (`#E5B869`, `#F5D084`):** The primary accent, reserved for celestial calculations, tradition anchors, ascendant nodes, and synthesized cultural concordances.
- **JPL Cyan (`#38BDF8`, `#0EA5E9`):** Secondary telemetry vector color. Used for NASA DE421/DE440 ephemeris feeds, raw right ascension/declination values, orbital angles, and active crosshair trackers.
- **Consensus Emerald (`#10B981`, `#34D399`):** Statistical validation markers indicating multi-system agreement, validated p-values, and Rodden AA rating certainty.
- **Sensitivity & Divergence Amber/Coral (`#F59E0B`, `#F43F5E`):** Critical alerts signaling cusp sensitivities, timing boundary errors, and inter-system model divergences.
- **Ice & Celestial Neutrals (`#F8FAFC`, `#94A3B8`, `#64748B`):** Pure white for primary data figures, degree-minute-second strings, and glyph metrics; muted blue-slates for parameter labels and telemetry units.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes clear boundaries between conceptual titles, narrative interpretation, and telemetry feeds.

- **Space Grotesk** commands display headers, mathematical model titles, and system designations. Its geometric, technical quirks provide institutional gravitas without falling into historical pastiche.
- **Inter** provides neutral legibility across astrological syntheses, multi-system comparative narratives, and parameter configuration fields.
- **JetBrains Mono** governs all numerical data: sexagesimal degrees, minutes, and seconds (`28°14'09"`); Julian day numbers; delta-t variations; Bayesian probability thresholds (`p < 0.0001`); and epistemic verification codes. Tabular figures (`tnum`) and slashed zeros must be enabled globally for this family.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an ultra-dense, responsive 12-column grid system modeled on multi-monitor laboratory telemetry consoles.

### Breakpoints & Reflow
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column split. Primary astrometric wheel/engine canvas occupies 7 columns; side telemetry rails, coordinate divergence matrices, and epistemic confidence readouts span the remaining 5 columns.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout. The astrometric interactive map remains sticky at the top half (or top viewport), collapsing comparative cultural data matrices into tabbed secondary surfaces.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column linear stack. Coordinate tables collapse into horizontally pannable monospaced cards with fixed column headers. Uncertainty bands switch from side-by-side comparative slots to stacked micro-gauges.

### Spatial Rhythm
Internal component paddings adhere to the 4px baseline rhythm (`space-xs` through `space-xl`). Modules favor compact vertical density (`space-sm` and `space-md`) to ensure critical celestial parameters appear above the fold without requiring excessive viewport scrolling.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a dark tonal stratification system combined with translucent laboratory slate surfaces and cold ambient illumination.

- **Level 0 (Deep Void Canvas):** `#080B10`. Monolithic background, optionally patterned with a faint, low-opacity (2%) astrometric equatorial coordinate grid.
- **Level 1 (Sub-surface Wells):** `#0D1118` with a 1px border of `#1E293B`. Used for inset data grids, tabular coordinate streams, and raw telemetry logs.
- **Level 2 (Observatory Panels):** `#161F2E` with 1px border of `#2B384E`. Standard container elevation. Carries a minimal ambient shadow (`0 8px 24px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`).
- **Level 3 (Interactive Overlays & Flyout Calibrators):** `#1E293B` at 92% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a high-precision hairline border (`#38BDF8` at 25% opacity or `#E5B869` at 30% opacity).
- **Glow & Starlight Radiance:** Rather than broad drop shadows, elevated focal elements utilize constrained, high-frequency outer glows (`0 0 16px rgba(229, 184, 105, 0.15)` for Starlight Brass; `0 0 16px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.18)` for JPL Cyan).

## Shapes

The interface adopts a **Soft Scientific** geometric profile (`roundedness: 1`). Radii remain small and precise, reflecting machined laboratory equipment rather than consumer software softness.

- Standard buttons, badges, and card containers use `0.25rem` (4px).
- Modals, large computational canvases, and system boundary enclosures use `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px).
- Sub-element pills (e.g., active culture tags, tradition markers) strictly maintain `rounded-xl` (`0.75rem` / 12px) or full pill geometry only when functioning as selectable tradition toggles.
- Hairline chamfered corner cuts (45-degree micro-notches) may be applied to top-level viewport header chips and telemetry badges to signal instrument calibration points.

## Components

### Buttons & Epistemic Action Controls
- **Primary Celestial Action:** Background `#E5B869`, text `#080B10`, font `Space Grotesk` (600 weight). Hover shifts to `#F5D084` with a subtle starlight halo (`0 0 12px rgba(229, 184, 105, 0.35)`). Active state applies a 1px scale inset.
- **Telemetry Action (Secondary):** Background transparent, border 1px solid `#38BDF8`, text `#38BDF8`. Hover fills to `#38BDF8` at 10% opacity with border brightening to `#0EA5E9`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** Background `#161F2E`, border 1px solid `#2B384E`, text `#94A3B8`. Hover text transitions to `#F8FAFC`.

### Precision Telemetry Badges
- Micro-containers displaying source verifications (e.g., `DE421 VERIFIED Δ≤0.003°`, `RODDEN AA`, `p < 0.0001`).
- Font: `JetBrains Mono`, size `telemetry-sm` (11px), uppercase.
- Structural anatomy: A 6px pulsing status dot positioned before the string, surrounded by a 1px slate frame with background `#0D1118`.
- Color tokens: Green dot (`#10B981`) for verified/high consensus; cyan dot (`#38BDF8`) for live orbital feed; amber dot (`#F59E0B`) for temporal uncertainty bands.

### Multi-System Tradition Selectors
- A segmented controller array housing the six canonical traditions: Jyotisha, Hellenistic, BaZi, Zi Wei, Maya, Tibetan.
- Inactive state: Borderless or muted border `#2B384E`, background `#0D1118`, text `#64748B`.
- Active state: Background `#1E293B`, border 1px solid `#E5B869`, text `#F8FAFC`, featuring a 2px brass indicator pill above or below the selection label.

### Ephemeris Coordinate Tables with Uncertainty Bands
- Tabular display showing nominal coordinate `t0`, plus sensitivity deviation tiers (`t-15m`, `t+15m`).
- All numbers render in `JetBrains Mono` with strict column alignment.
- When coordinate boundaries shift zodiacal sign or mansion within the delta window, the cell renders an amber outline (`#F59E0B`) with an inline warning icon and micro-label `BOUNDARY SENSITIVE`.

### Consensus & Divergence Radial Meters
- Circular and linear gauges indicating percentage of epistemic cross-system alignment (0% to 100%).
- Track background: `#1E293B`.
- Active gauge track: `#10B981` (Consensus ≥ 80%), `#E5B869` (Partial Consensus 50–79%), `#F43F5E` (High Divergence < 50%).
- Center readout: Space Grotesk headline accompanied by a monospaced delta variance indicator (`σ = 0.04`).

### Input Fields & Temporal Epoch Controls
- Inset slate background (`#0D1118`), 1px border `#2B384E`.
- Focus state: Border shifts to JPL Cyan (`#38BDF8`) with zero blur displacement; text color `#F8FAFC`.
- Integrated suffix badges for latitude, longitude, elevation, and Julian day calculations with copy-to-clipboard coordinate strings.
